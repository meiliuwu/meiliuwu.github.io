**Hanyi Xiong (Undergraduate student)**

I am currently a year three undergraduate student majoring in Applied AI from the University of Hong Kong. My research is positioned at the intersection of GeoAI, multimodal learning, and computer vision. I focus on building comprehensive multimodal models that integrate diverse modalities such as text, imagery, and spatial coordinates, aiming to extract richer and more holistic geospatial information. My research explores how generative AI and contrastive learning can be leveraged to align multiple perspectives—ranging from street-view and aerial imagery to geospatial data—in order to enhance spatial understanding and geolocalization tasks. A central direction of my work is adapting foundation models and utilizing GPS as a semantic bridge to align disparate views, enabling robust representation learning even with limited paired data.

My long-term research goal is to develop more precise and complete GeoAI-empowered multimodal foundation models. By advancing multimodal cross-view learning and integrating diverse spatial perspectives, I aim to contribute to urban analytics and environmental applications, ultimately fostering geospatial AI systems that are accurate, generalizable, and socially impactful.
