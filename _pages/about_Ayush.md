I have completed my BS-MS dual degree in Data Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Bhopal. I was working with Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) imagery, satellite data, and street view imagery at the GeoAI4Cities Lab at IISER-B under the supervision of Dr. Vaibhav Kumar. 

Currently, I am a first-year PhD in Geospatial Data Science at the University of Glasgow. My primary research objective is to address complex urban planning challenges by developing data-driven policy tools. I **leverage geospatial multimodal datasets (e.g., geo-tagged images and sounds) and devleop a range of deep learning techniques for cross-modality generation**. The ultimate aim of my work is to contribute to sustainable urban development, enhancing the resilience of our cities and aligning them more effectively with the evolving needs of our society.
