### Haiyu Zhang (Visiting PhD student)

I am a PhD student in Geography at Tianjin Normal University, visiting GIFTS Lab. My research examines **how overseas industrial parks develop connections with their surrounding regions and wider international networks**, with a focus on the China-Egypt TEDA Suez Economic and Trade Cooperation Zone.

I plan to combine GIS and remote sensing with analysis of interviews, policy documents and historical materials to explore how changes in land use, infrastructure and connectivity relate to the park's development. I am also interested in how actors, institutions and power relations shape regional and transnational connections. Through this work, I aim to understand what these relationships mean for the sustainable development of overseas cooperation zones and to inform the planning of similar zones.
