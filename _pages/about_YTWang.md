### Yueting Wang (PhD student)

My PhD research focuses on **mapping and monitoring coastal vegetated ecosystems with geospatial AI**. I plan to use AlphaEarth Foundations satellite embeddings, which summarise information from Earth observation data, to study mangroves, salt marshes, seagrass beds and coastal shrublands.

I will compare machine learning and deep learning approaches for mapping these habitats and detecting change, and assess their reliability against independent inventories and field observations, including work in Scottish estuaries. I aim to make model uncertainty visible and develop accessible maps and analysis tools that support coastal conservation, restoration and biodiversity monitoring.
