### Mengkun Song (PhD student)

My PhD research explores **how people's perceptions of streetscapes change across space and time in global cities**. I plan to combine time-series street view imagery with multimodal large language models to assess changes in perceptions such as safety, beauty and vitality.

I aim to test how reliably these models assess streetscape change, examining the effects of prompting, bias and uncertainty. I will analyse how perception changes relate to the built environment and socioeconomic context across cities and neighbourhoods, and compare relevant urban policies and plans. The project aims to inform people-centred urban planning and streetscape improvements that respond to local conditions.
