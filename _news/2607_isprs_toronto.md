I attended the **XXV ISPRS Congress** in **Toronto, Canada, from 4–11 July 2026**, together with **Zhimeng He**, a PhD student I co-supervise. It was a pleasure to share this conference experience with Zhimeng and see our group’s research represented in an international community spanning photogrammetry, remote sensing and spatial information science.

Our contributions included work on spatial intelligence in vision-language models, building detection and image super-resolution. Two papers from our group appeared in the ISPRS Annals, with Zhimeng contributing to both and leading the **Cube Kernel** paper:

- **From Super-Resolution to Superior Land-Cover Detection: Cross-Channel Attention Network for Aerial Images**, by Yuwei Cai, Zhimeng He, Meiliu Wu and Brian Barrett. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-annals-XI-3-2026-515-2026).
- **Cube Kernel: A Novel Approach to Enable Local Gradient Flow Across Channels in CNNs**, by Zhimeng He, Yuwei Cai, Meiliu Wu, X. Xian and Brian Barrett. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-annals-XI-3-2026-109-2026).

My warm thanks to the **ISPRS Congress organising committee and session chairs** for bringing the community together and creating such a welcoming setting for presentations and discussion. Thank you also to the colleagues who stopped to exchange ideas with us.
