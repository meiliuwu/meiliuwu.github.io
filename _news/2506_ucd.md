Huge thanks to Dr Ye Tian for being such a wonderful host during my visit to the School of Geography at UCD during Jun 12-13, 2025! I truly appreciated the warm welcome, the thought-provoking conversations, and especially the chance to engage with such curious and enthusiastic students. Their genuine interest in my work—and in the growing field of GeoAI—made the visit all the more rewarding! 

---

> "This talk presents an innovative GeoAI framework designed to seamlessly integrate urban POIs, street view, satellite imagery, and 3D data through geospatial alignment, facilitating the fine-tuning of foundational models (FMs). By embedding geospatial intelligence into these models, the framework significantly enhances their capability to perform complex urban analytical tasks, such as visual reconstruction and retrieval, semantic segmentation, and spatial-context aware text generation."
