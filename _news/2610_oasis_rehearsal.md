Our **two accepted OASIS 2026 papers** explore **GeoAI agents for flood resilience and public health equity**: **HydroMind (Track A · Disaster Resilience & Vulnerability Analysis)** and **GeoMind (Track B · Public Health & Spatial Equity)**. GeoMind focuses on uncertainty-aware and equitable siting of public health services with a human in the loop.

On **3 November 2026**, **Yueting Wang and Eleanor Downie** presented **HydroMind**, one of **ten finalists** in the [OASIS Student Challenge](https://rsvp.withgoogle.com/events/oasis-2026) at **ACM SIGSPATIAL 2026** in **Riverside, California**.

In their talk and live demo, they showed how HydroMind, a **GeoAI agent**, uses **open geospatial data** to map flood risk across **Glasgow**. The assessment follows the **IPCC’s risk framework**, bringing together **flood hazard, exposure and vulnerability**. Its **human-in-the-loop approach** lets users adjust weights to explore which neighbourhoods take priority, supporting different decisions by **residents, emergency responders, transport authorities and businesses**.

Yueting and Eleanor also attended talks in the main conference’s **Research and Application sessions**. At the **Demo + Posters sessions**, they explored geospatial research projects and discussed methods and findings with the authors. **This was their first academic conference as PhD students.** I’m so proud of their work and delighted that they could share HydroMind with the SIGSPATIAL community!

[Explore HydroMind](https://ytwang-79.github.io/HydroMind/) and view the [paper DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3849739.3856758) (link currently pending).

## Presentation rehearsal · 28 October 2026

On **Wednesday, 28 October 2026**, our team came together to rehearse presentations for **two accepted OASIS 2026 papers**. The two projects explore how GeoAI agents can support decisions for social good, from flood risk assessment to equitable public health services.

### HydroMind: A GeoAI Agent Framework for Open Geospatial Flood Risk Assessment

**Track A · Disaster Resilience & Vulnerability Analysis**

**Team HydroMind:** Yueting Wang, Eleanor Downie, Quangao Liu and Meiliu Wu.

Our accepted paper presents a GeoAI agent framework for open geospatial flood risk assessment. [DOI: 10.1145/3849739.3856758](https://doi.org/10.1145/3849739.3856758) (link currently pending).

### A Human-in-the-Loop GeoAI Agent for Uncertainty-Aware and Equitable Siting in Public Health Services

**Track B · Public Health & Spatial Equity**

**Team GeoMind:** Fengjiao Li, Ayush Dabra, Yueting Wang and Meiliu Wu.

Our accepted paper focuses on a human-in-the-loop GeoAI agent for uncertainty-aware and equitable siting of public health services. [DOI: 10.1145/3849739.3856757](https://doi.org/10.1145/3849739.3856757) (forthcoming link).

Congratulations to both teams on their accepted papers! A wonderful group meal followed the rehearsal, a lovely way to celebrate the work and spend time together before the conference.
