#GISRUK Thrilled to have had the opportunity to present our work on "Towards GeoAI Multimodal Foundation Models: Unlocking Spatial Knowledge through Vision-Language Models" as well as chairing the session on "GeoAI & Large Language Models" in GISRUK 2025. I was fully immersed in high-intensity exchanges! 

Special thanks to the School of Geographical Sciences at the University of Bristol, including the local organising committee: 
Levi John Wolf, Rui Zhu, Caitlin Robinson, Lenka Hasova, Rich Harris, Qiujie Shi, Emmanouil Tranos, Ce Zhang, and Richard Timmerman.
