I joined the **Workshop on GeoAI for Autonomous Spatial Analysis** at the **University of Maryland, College Park, on 14–15 June 2026**, held in conjunction with the 2026 UCGIS Annual Symposium.

My invited talk, **“Teaching GeoAI to Know Its SPLACE: Spatially explicit multimodal learning + PLACES evaluation”**, explored spatially explicit multimodal learning and evaluation for autonomous spatial analysis. It was a pleasure to exchange ideas with colleagues across GIScience and AI.

Thank you to the **workshop organising committee and session chairs** for the invitation, thoughtful discussions and warm welcome, and to our hosts at the University of Maryland for bringing everyone together.

[UCGIS Symposium at Maryland](https://calendar.umd.edu/ai-everywhere-ucgis-symposium)

## Conversations and reunions

Beyond the workshop sessions, I enjoyed reconnecting with **old friends and colleagues from UCGIS**. We shared many meals, caught up with one another and continued our conversations around the table. These reunions made the trip especially memorable—thank you to everyone who made time to meet.
