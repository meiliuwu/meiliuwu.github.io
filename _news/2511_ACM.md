Thrilled to have had the opportunity to attend ACM SIGSPATIAL 2025, hosted at University of Minnesota, 03-06 Nov, 2025. Special thanks to the organising committee! 

We published two full papers: 

1.	**Han T.**, Chen H., Wang C., Ren Y., **Wu M.@**, 2025. Towards A New Era of Geo-Foundation Models: Expert-Guided Multimodal Alignment and Geospatial Context Awareness. In Proceedings of ACM SIGSPATIAL 2025 (acceptance rate 25%), Nov 3-6, Minneapolis, MN, USA. DOI: 10.1145/3748636.3762756 

2.	Chen H.#, **Han T.#**, Chen S., Guo Z., Chen Y., **Wu M.@**, 2025. Semantic4Safety: Causal Insights from Zero-shot Street View Imagery Segmentation for Urban Road Safety. In Proceedings of the 8th ACM SIGSPATIAL International Workshop on AI for Geographic Knowledge Discovery (GeoAI ’25), ACM SIGSPATIAL 2025, Nov 3-6, Minneapolis, MN, USA. DOI: 10.1145/3764912.3770830
