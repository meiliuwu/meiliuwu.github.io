**Date**: 14 – 15 July 2025 

**Location**: Z205, Block Z, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University 

**Organiser**: Research Centre for Artificial Intelligence in Geomatics and Jockey Club STEM Lab of Earth Observations at PolyU

---

Thrilled to have had the opportunity to be invited for a guest talk "AI Meets Geography: Embedding Geospatial Intelligence into Vision-Language Models" and to chair the Technical Session II at the 2025 Workshop and Tutorial in GeoAI Advancements and Applications at PolyU! 

Special thanks to Prof. Qihao Weng for the invitation! I truly appreciated the warm welcome and the thought-provoking conversations with such curious and enthusiastic colleagues and students!
