I attended the first **International Conference on Geospatial Artificial Intelligence (GeoAI 2026)** in **Ghent, Belgium, from 3–6 June 2026**, together with my PhD student **Fengjiao Li**. It was lovely to share the experience with Fengjiao and to see her present our work on **spatially explicit graph neural networks for epidemic forecasting**.

I **led and organised the workshop Building Open Labs and Learning Activities for “Introduction to Geospatial AI”**, with the help of **Profs. Siqin Wang and Song Gao**. I am very grateful to both for their support in bringing the workshop together. I also served on the conference programme committee. Our research contributions included:

- **Spatially Explicit Graph Neural Networks for Epidemic Forecasting: Evidence from Socioeconomic Embeddings**, by Fengjiao Li, Meiliu Wu and Ana Basiri. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19475485).
- **Spatially-Weighted CLIP for Street-View Geo-localization**, by Ting Han and colleagues. [Preprint](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.04357).

My sincere thanks to the **GeoAI 2026 organising committee and session chairs** for making this first conference such a welcoming place to share ideas. I am also grateful to our fellow workshop organisers and participants for their contributions to the GeoAI teaching discussions.

[Conference website](https://geoaiconference.org/)
