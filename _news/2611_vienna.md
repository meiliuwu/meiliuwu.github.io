On **11 November 2026**, I visited [**Prof Krzysztof Janowicz (“Jano”)**](https://ufind.univie.ac.at/en/person.html?id=1001662) and his research team at the **University of Vienna, Austria**. It was a pleasure to share my research in a talk titled **“Where Is the Geo in GeoAI?”**

My talk explored how **proximity, connectivity, scale and local context** can shape GeoAI foundation models, and how **multimodal learning** brings together different views of place through imagery, LiDAR, points of interest and text. I also discussed evaluating spatial reasoning across places and times, with uncertainty and human oversight in GeoAI agents and digital twins.

We had a lively discussion about **foundation models, multimodal data and learning**, including how AI understands linguistic meanings related to **space and time**. We also exchanged ideas about geospatial applications in **environmental monitoring, natural hazards and digital twins**. It was exciting to explore these questions together and hear different perspectives on future research.

**Thank you, Jano and the whole team, for your warm hospitality, thoughtful questions and generous conversations!** The visit was especially meaningful because I could also reconnect with **my PhD advisor**, who is currently on sabbatical in Vienna and working with Jano’s group. I was so happy to see my advisor again and share ideas in such a welcoming research environment.
