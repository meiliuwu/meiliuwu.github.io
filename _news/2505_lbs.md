#LBS2025 Thrilled to have had the opportunity to present our work “Advancing Vision-Language Models with Spatial-Context Prompt Tuning: A Case Study in GeoAI-Empowered Image Geo-Localization”, to attend the <a href="https://ptal-io.github.io/ddma2025/">Data-Driven Mobility Analytics Workshop</a>, and to chair the session on “GeoAI and LBS” at LBS 2025, hosted on the beautiful campus of Aalto University, Finland.

The conference was both rewarding and enjoyable — from the inspiring talks and high-energy exchanges to the endless buffets (and relaxing saunas!). 

Special thanks to the Conference Chairs: Juha Oksanen (FGI), Henrikki Tenkanen (Aalto University), and Pyry Kettunen (FGI) for their excellent organization and warm hospitality. 

Also, huge thanks to the workshop organizers: Henrikki Tenkanen, Aalto Univerity, Finland; Marina Toger, University of Uppsala, Sweden; and Grant McKenzie, McGill University, Canada.
